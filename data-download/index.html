Fields and provenance

Study records include PMID, title, authors, journal, year, DOI, publication types, MeSH headings, topic membership, design classification and primary-source URL. Trial records include NCT ID, status, enrollment, conditions, interventions, dates and source URL.

HRD republishes metadata, not full journal articles. Original source rights remain with their owners.
