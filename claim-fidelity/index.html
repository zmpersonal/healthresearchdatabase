Claim Fidelity

Claim Fidelity asks whether cited research actually matches the words in a health claim. A large literature can coexist with poor claim fidelity.

Eight dimensions

What HRD compares

InterventionFinnish sauna ≠ infrared sauna
PopulationHeart-failure patients ≠ healthy adults
DoseDaily protocol ≠ occasional use
DurationAcute response ≠ durable benefit
ComparatorNo treatment ≠ active treatment
OutcomeSoreness ≠ muscle recovery
MeasurementBiomarker ≠ clinical outcome
SettingSupervised study ≠ home device
Ratings

High, moderate or low

High means the central intervention and outcome closely match. Moderate signals material differences or incomplete reporting. Low means the claim substantially extrapolates beyond what was studied. Fidelity does not grade study quality or effectiveness.

Example

Research depth and fidelity are separate

Traditional sauna may have a large cardiovascular literature while a claim about infrared-sauna longevity has low fidelity. The broader research volume does not repair the modality mismatch.
