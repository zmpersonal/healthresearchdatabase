Funding & Conflicts

Transparency matters more than manufactured neutrality. This page records the relationship between HRD and its operator's commercial interests.

Funding

How the project is supported

HRD is independently operated and currently supported by its owner. It does not accept payment to change a claim verdict, include a study or alter a Research Receipt.

Commercial relationship

InHouse Wellness

The site operator has a commercial relationship with InHouse Wellness, a retailer of saunas, cold plunges and related wellness equipment. Research pages are separated from the single retailer-information page, and no product shopping links appear within claim or evidence pages.

Conflicts policy

What the separation requires

Commerce does not determine inclusion criteria, study classification, Claim Fidelity or editorial conclusions. Potential conflicts should be disclosed on the relevant governance pages.
