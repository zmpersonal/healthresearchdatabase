About Health Research Database

Health Research Database is a public, continuously updated map between consumer health claims, human research records and registered clinical trials.

Purpose

Why HRD exists

Research is often compressed into the phrase “studies show.” HRD makes the missing details visible: which studies, what intervention, what outcome, what changed and how closely the source evidence matches the claim.

Scope

Focused before broad

The first release concentrates on sauna and passive heat, cold-water immersion, contrast therapy, photobiomodulation, floatation and massage. HRD deepens these topics before expanding indiscriminately.

Independence

Research first

Claim, topic, study and trial pages contain no shopping links. Commercial relationships and project funding are disclosed separately.
