Does massage help lower-back pain?

May help short-term symptoms

Massage may reduce pain and improve function for some people with nonspecific lower-back pain, particularly in the short term. Effects vary by technique, treatment schedule, comparator and whether massage is combined with exercise or usual care. Evidence does not establish one best protocol or a durable benefit for everyone.
