Does passive heating improve sleep?

Promising, protocol-sensitive

Passive heating before bedtime may improve sleep onset or subjective sleep quality in some studies, but timing and temperature matter. Evidence from warm baths, hot-water immersion and sauna should not be collapsed into a single protocol, and the small research base does not establish a universal effect.
