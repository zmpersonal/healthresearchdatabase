Do saunas increase longevity?

Association, not proof

Frequent sauna bathing has been associated with lower cardiovascular and all-cause mortality in observational research, but that does not prove sauna itself extends life. People who use saunas frequently may differ in health, income, activity and other ways. Randomized trials have not established a longevity benefit.

Does the research match the words?

The best-known mortality evidence concerns Finnish sauna bathing. It cannot automatically support claims about infrared saunas, occasional use or different temperatures and session lengths.
