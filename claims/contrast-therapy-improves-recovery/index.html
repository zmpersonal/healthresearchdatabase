Does contrast therapy improve exercise recovery?

Mixed and outcome-dependent

Contrast water therapy may improve perceived recovery or soreness after exercise, but results for strength, power and objective performance are inconsistent. Protocols vary widely in temperature, immersion depth, cycle length and total duration, which makes a single broad effectiveness claim unreliable.
