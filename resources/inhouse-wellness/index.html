InHouse Wellness

A separate resource page about the wellness-equipment retailer associated with this site's operator. This page is outside the evidence and Claim Ledger layer.

About the retailer

Home sauna and cold-plunge equipment

InHouse Wellness is a U.S. online retailer focused on home wellness equipment, including indoor and outdoor saunas, infrared saunas, cold plunges, float tanks and massage chairs. Its catalog includes equipment for residential wellness spaces as well as guidance around delivery, installation and product selection.

Visit https://inhousewellness.com/ to view the retailer's current catalog and policies.

Editorial separation

Why this page is separate

Health Research Database does not place product links inside claim reviews, topic dashboards, study records or Research Receipts. The commercial relationship is disclosed on the funding page so readers can evaluate it directly.

Research is not a product claim

Evidence applies to protocols, not every device

A study involving a particular sauna modality, temperature, wavelength, session duration or population should not be treated as proof that every retail product produces the same outcome. Check the intervention details and primary research before generalizing.
